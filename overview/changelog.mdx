This page lists updates and improvements to bleed, with the newest changes first.

## November 7, 2026 -- Saturday Night Update

### New Features

- You can now customize bleed's namestyle with `/config customize namestyle`!
- Crypto got a big upgrade:
  - `/crypto alert` DMs you when a coin goes above or below a price.
  - `/crypto convert` converts between coins and currencies.
  - `/crypto chart` draws a candlestick chart with period buttons.
  - `/crypto fees` shows Bitcoin network fees.
  - `/crypto address` looks up a wallet's balance and latest transactions.
  - `/crypto top` shows the biggest coins and today's movers.
- `/crypto subscribe` now works for Litecoin and Ethereum, can wait for up to 50 confirmations, and can tell you by DM.
- Confessions are here! Members post with `/confess`, from their DMs with the bot or in server. Use `/confessions config` to get started.
- `/filter names` cleans fancy text like `𝓭𝓮𝓻𝓻𝓲𝓬𝓴` and hoisting symbols out of member names, automatically or all at once with `/filter names cleanup`.
- Counters got a big upgrade:
  - Name counters with variables like `{guild.count}`.
  - Show a crypto price in 15 currencies.
  - Set goals and post milestones to a channel.
  - See one counter's settings with `/automation counter config`.
- Counting now has a don't-reset option, pinned numbers and role rewards.
- `/logs forum` make a forum post for each kind of log event, all on one webhook.
- `/logs message` change a log post's opening message to any message, embed or components script.
- `/channel copyperms` copies one channel's permissions onto another, replacing them or merging with `merge:`.
- `/music config` puts the DJ role, volume, autoplay and a new Max Bitrate switch on one card.
- Claimed tickets can keep trainees visible with the new Keep Trainees Visible On Claim toggle.
- Ticket support roles can now manage the ticket blacklist.
- Ticket logs can now go to a thread post.

### Changed Behavior

- `/queue view` got a whole new look.
- Names cleaned by the name filter show up in your Member Updated log with the reason "Name Filter".
- Timed tempbans, mutes and jails are limited to a year.
- Reposts and feeds show up to ten images.

### Bug Fixes

- Fixed ten ticket bugs, including:
  - Support roles losing a ticket after an unclaim.
  - A full Closed or Claimed category stopping a close or claim.
  - Double presses of the ticket buttons.
- A components ticket panel no longer loses everything but its dropdown when a ticket form is sent.
- A button role removed by editing its message can be added back now.
- `/queue rewind` and `/queue fastforward` now properly move from the current position.
- `{user.display_name}` and `{moderator.display_name}` give the display name in punishment messages.
- Bump reminders are working again after DISBOARD changed its bump reply.

## October 31, 2026 -- Halloween Update

### New Features

- Custom messages got a big upgrade. You can now use Components V2 scripting anywhere you set a custom message, including buttons with replies, welcome messages, tags, tickets and `/fm`.
- Build, save, preview and edit components messages with `/config component`.
- `/ce` creates embeds or components messages, takes file uploads, and can convert your old embed code.
- Broken script? The bot now lists every error, suggests a fix, and can apply it for you with the Implement fix button.
- Dropdown roles are finally here, with up to 25 roles per menu, exclusive mode and pick limits.
- Required roles let you lock reaction roles, button roles and dropdown menus behind other roles.
- Button roles now work on components messages, and you can choose where each button goes.
- New `voice_members` counter that shows how many people are in voice across your server.
- Counting is here! Use `/game counting` and try to beat your server's high score.
- Play Connect 4 with `/game connect4`, with stats and a leaderboard.
- `/utility todo` gives you a private to-do list with priorities, notes and an undo button.
- `/automation leaderboard` posts message and voice leaderboards for the specified timeframe.
- Two new right-click apps: Quote and Purge Up to Message.
- Filters now run on points. Set up your punishment ladder with `/filter points`.
- `/lookup scores` covers NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL, soccer, college sports, tennis, golf, UFC, Formula 1 and NASCAR.
- `/search` now has 19 engines, and `/search chatgpt` can read images.
- Manage your server's soundboard with `/config soundboard add` and `/config soundboard remove`.
- `/mute image` and `/mute reaction` are now in slash, and `/case` keeps your case notes and proof in one place.
- Your `/fm` can now be a components script with buttons.
- Antinuke and antiraid actions now show up in your mod log.
- Give `/emotes` two emoji and Emoji Kitchen will combine them.
- Giveaways can now hide their winners when the draw ends with `/giveaways edit hidewinners`.
- `run` now figures out your language for you, and it supports 19 of them.

### Changed Behavior

- bleed has a fresh new look. Status replies, lists, the music queue, `/userinfo`, and avatar, banner and icon replies now use Components V2 instead of traditional embeds.
- Filters are slash-only now, so every `,filter` command points you to `/filter`.
- Some slash commands have new homes: playback controls like pause, resume, shuffle and repeat are under `/queue`, tickets are under `/tickets`, crypto is under `/crypto`, and lookups like `/xbox` and `/steam` are under `/lookup profile`.
- The VoiceMaster activity button was replaced with a music mode toggle.
- `/nowplaying` is now `/fm` and `/lastfm now`.
- Prefix and slash commands now share cooldowns.
- Last.fm library updates are much faster.

### Bug Fixes

- Instagram feeds are posting to your channels again.
- `/image` is back to using Google Images.
- `/search reverseimage` is working again.
- Fixed an issue where variables could be used to inject unwanted user content into messages.
- Fixed a bug where giveaways started with slash commands could draw twice, picking two sets of winners and handing out award roles twice.
